During the last semeser of college, our Final was to make our own work of art, with the caviat that it would be put in a digital museum. My work entitled liberators ofthe land was reflecting on the issue at the time which was the state of Afganistan since the Taliban takeover. Concived of in January, but midway in prodcution, the Ukrainian War had begun. Thus the Media attention was quickly on that instead of the curent Afghani issue still in affect. By the time the work was completed, I felt that it had both more weight and urgency to the subject matter. However, due to a scedualing error the final complete work never made it into the Digital Museum, leaving the picture looking compressed and flat instead of curved and clear.
